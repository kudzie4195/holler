Each card that comes from a public record links to its public source. A permit does not tell you whether the owner has hired anyone. A violation or a failed inspection is on the record, but whether the owner is hiring is not known. A 311 request is what someone reported, and the record does not show the city confirmed it. A bid listing does not show who may respond or who will be chosen. A new business or a property sale is on the record, but the need for work is not known. A request or a public post is in its writer's own words, and Holler has not checked them. A weather alert or a storm report only records the event: damage is not confirmed.
